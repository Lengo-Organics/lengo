The Founder

From a personal search to an African house of beauty

Lengo means “beauty” in Luo — a word carried from founder Samantha Okullo's Northern Ugandan heritage, and her conviction that beauty is balance: how we care for ourselves, and how we show up in the world.

What began in 2019 as a search for better options for textured hair became a Ugandan-made house built on rare Nilotica shea, botanical precision, and an ambition to place African luxury on the global stage.

Samantha

Made with heritage, precision, and purpose.

Lengo means "beauty" in Luo, reflecting founder Samantha Okullo's Northern Ugandan heritage and her belief that beauty is balance — the connection between how we care for ourselves and how we show up in the world.

Founded in 2019, Lengo Organics grew from a personal search for better options for textured hair and scalp needs into a Ugandan-made hair care brand centered on organic, shea butter-based products.

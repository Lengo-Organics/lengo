Founder Spotlight

Samantha Okullo

Founder of Lengo Organics · Owner of Mirrors Unisex Salon, So Chic Custom Wigs & Mirrors Beauty School — building a Uganda-made beauty ecosystem from the ground up.

Read Her Story Follow Her Journey

Born

November 1994, Uganda.

Heritage

Luo roots (Northern Uganda, father's side) and Runyankole/Rukiga roots (Western Uganda, mother's side).

Education

Uganda Management Institute (UMI), graduated March 2024 — research focused on urban governance and municipal solid waste management within the KCCA framework.

Founded

Mirrors Unisex Salon, 2016, at age 21 — the first venture in what's now a multi-brand beauty ecosystem.

The Builder

Given a start, then built the rest herself.

Samantha Okullo opened Mirrors Unisex Salon in 2016 at 21, with early backing from her father. What followed was seven years of running the business, largely self-taught, before Mirrors grew into the flagship of a wider ecosystem — and before Lengo Organics, her own product line, was born in 2019.

She's been open about where the confidence to keep building it came from.

"He only gave it to me because he could see the vision I had. I had to prove to him that it was going to work." Samantha Okullo, on her father's early support — SatisFashion Uganda

In Her Own Words

Recognition

Named to the Africa 100 League of Exceptionals.

In April 2025, Samantha was honoured among the 100 Most Influential Young Africans at the Africa 100 League of Exceptionals, hosted by the Pan-African Youth Leadership Foundation at the British Council in Accra, Ghana.

The recognition follows years of building Uganda's beauty sector from the ground up — training and employing over 400 young women across her ventures, and growing So Chic Custom Wigs into a sustainability-minded initiative recognised by Airtel Uganda's Young Hustlers Innovation Program for recycling hair extensions into new units.

She's also been nominated for the Abryanz Style & Fashion Awards, the Hair & Beauty Awards and the FOYA Digitally Fit Awards, and was featured in Uganda Airlines' Ngaali in-flight magazine in September 2024 as one of Uganda's Top 5 Female Beauty Entrepreneurs.

In Her Own Words

Comfort first, colour on holiday.

Asked about her personal style, Samantha keeps it practical: comfort for work, "high heels, accessories and colour" when she's off the clock — and a hard line against fake designer labels in favour of genuine African-made fashion.

  • Go-to silhouetteWide-legged pants.
  • Won't be caught wearingCounterfeit designer labels — she shops authentic Ugandan and East African designers instead.
  • Favourite brandsStudio Fit Kenya, Miss Shee, STW UG, and Shwanda Kollection for jewellery.

Source: "Style: Samantha Okullo, Influencer," Sqoop Uganda, March 2024.

"Beauty is Balance."

A lesson from her mother — that you can't be beautiful on the outside while carrying bad manners on the inside. It's the philosophy behind Lengo Organics as much as it is her own.
